## About the Game

**Legenda Lapangan** ("Legend of the Pitch") is a football player career simulation. You create your own player: position, play style, skill perks, preferred foot, birthplace and heritage. Then you live your career week by week: train, play, change clubs, represent your country, and finally retire as a legend.

Every match can be **played live** on a 3D pitch with touch controls, or finished with a **quick simulation**. Decisions off the pitch, such as contracts, your relationship with the coach, social media, and your personal life, shape where your career goes.

## Main Features

### Create Your Own Player
- Step-by-step creation: Identity, Position, Perks, Attributes, then Origin and Club
- **15 detailed positions** (ST, CF, LW, RW, CAM, CM, CDM, LB, CB, RB, GK, and more) picked on a pitch diagram, plus up to **2 alternate positions**
- **Play styles** per position, such as Goal Poacher, Creative Playmaker, Box-to-Box, Destroyer, and Sweeper Keeper
- **Skill perks** such as Power Shot, Cheetah, Trickster, Incisive Pass, Aerial, and Cat Reflexes. Up to 3 at the start, with extra slots at ratings 70, 80, and 88
- Preferred foot, skill-move stars, and weak-foot stars
- **Player card** with a 7-attribute radar, fitness, sharpness, morale, and attribute gains through the season

### Live Matches
- 3D matches with a **Close** camera (mobile game style) and a **Broadcast** camera (TV style)
- You control one player; the other 21 are controlled by the computer
- **Sides switch** at half-time, **added time** based on how the match went, and a calm referee
- **Match sounds**: referee whistle, a crowd that rises when the ball nears goal, goal cheers
- **Broadcast-style scoreboards** for the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, World Cup, Asian Cup, and AFC Champions League Elite
- **Home, away, and third kits** with no colour clashes, selectable before kick-off
- **Shot power**: hold a button to charge from green (soft) to red (hard), adjusted by your stats
- Realistic ball physics: ground passes slow down, lofted passes follow gravity, over-hit passes can bounce off the receiver
- Passes, through balls, lofted passes and crosses, shots, and **skill moves based on joystick direction** (knock-on, step-over, roulette, elastico)
- Defend with tackles, slide tackles, jockeying, **team pressing**, blocks, and auto-pressing
- Step-by-step set pieces: corners, free kicks, penalties, goal kicks, throw-ins, and offside
- **Opponent AI difficulty** Easy / Medium / Hard: on Easy, opponents are slower and tackle less while your teammates stay normal
- Adjustable scoring difficulty, game speed, and graphics; supports 30 to 120 Hz screens

### A Living Football World
- **Three-tier leagues** in many countries, with promotion and relegation
- **Champions League, Europa League, Conference League** with a **36-team league phase** (1st-8th go straight to the round of 16, 9th-24th to the knockout play-offs), two-legged quarter-finals and semi-finals, and an **intro video** before the opening match
- **AFC Champions League Elite**, other continental competitions, the **FIFA Club World Cup**, and the **Intercontinental Cup**
- **Season calendar** like in FIFA: see every match by month, simulate to a chosen match or to the last one
- AI players who develop, age, and retire, each with their own play style and perks
- Many formations and a different **coaching strategy** for every team

### Training and Development
- **Training drills**: finishing, passing, dribbling, sprints, endurance, defending, tactics, weak foot, skill moves, and goalkeeping
- Each drill uses a different amount of energy
- **Elite Gym** for extra training every week

### Career and Transfers
- Contracts, extensions, wage negotiation, and your own wage requests, with signed **contract documents**
- **Two transfer windows**: winter (December) and summer (after the season ends)
- **Coach's office**: ask for a raise or more playing time
- **Realistic loans**: you are only loaned out if your form declines for two seasons in a row. Shine at the loan club and it may buy you permanently as a starter
- **Player agents**, a market value that rises and falls, and custom shirt numbers

### National Team and Awards
- Choose your country of birth or **heritage** (mixed heritage, up to 3 nations)
- National call-ups based on FIFA ranking, form, and reputation
- Tournaments: **World Cup, Asian Cup, Nations League, ASEAN Cup**
- Awards: Player of the Season, Young Player of the Season, **Ballon d'Or**, **Puskas Award**, and a personal trophy cabinet

### Life off the Pitch
- **Social media** in the style of Instagram and TikTok; upload your own photos from the gallery
- **News** covering debuts, hat-tricks, records, transfer rumours, and online criticism
- **Romance and marriage**, DMs, family, children, and a **club academy** for children aged 6 and up
- **Lifestyle**: houses, cars, watches, art, boats, private jets, properties, **businesses** with weekly income, and **charity donations**

### End of Career
- Retire from age 30 (automatically at 40)
- A full career summary and a title from *Professional Player* up to **World Legend**

### Language, Music, and Saves
- **English and Indonesian**, chosen on first launch
- Menu music: *Lost Sky - Where We Started (feat. Jex) [NCS Release]*, or the built-in synth music
- **5 save slots**, and saves can be exported and imported as text

## Controls

Button labels change with the situation: when you have the ball, when a teammate has it, and when defending.

| Button | On the ball | Teammate on the ball | Defending | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Joystick | Move and aim | Move | Move | `WASD` / Arrows |
| Pass | Ground pass (hold for power) | Call for the ball | Tackle | `J` |
| Through | Through ball | Ask for a through ball | Contain (hold to jockey) | `I` |
| Lob | Lofted pass or cross | Ask for a high ball | Team press | `U` |
| Shoot | Shot (hold for power) | Run into space | Slide tackle | `K` |
| Skill | Skill move by joystick direction | Call for the ball | Block | `L` |
| Sprint | Sprint | Sprint | Sprint | `Shift` |
| Pause | Pause menu | Pause menu | Pause menu | `P` |

A quick tap on a kick button uses automatic power. Joystick type, button size and opacity, auto-pressing, and game speed can be changed in **Settings > Controls**.

## Customization (Customization Pro)

Build your own fan-made version without touching the game's default data. Open **Customization** from the main menu.

- **Clubs**: name, scoreboard code, logo, colours, coach, and players (name, OVR, age, position)
- **Nations**: logo, code, national coach, and national team players
- **Partners**: your own romance candidates (name, age 18 or older, profession, origin, photo) who appear in the Romance menu
- **Assets**: asset names and images

Everything can be edited on screen (the **Edit** and **Change logo** buttons) or with a JSON file. Logos and images are replaced on import too:

1. **One JSON file** that already contains the images (created with the **Export database** button).
2. **JSON + images**: pick the JSON file and all images at once. Write the image name in the JSON (`"clubLogo": "rma.png"`) or name each image after the club id, nation name, or asset id.
3. **A ZIP file** containing the JSON and its images.

```json
{
  "format": "legenda-lapangan-db",
  "version": 1,
  "name": "My Database",
  "author": "Your Name",
  "clubs": [
    {
      "id": "rma",
      "clubName": "Los Blancos FC",
      "clubLogo": "rma.png",
      "clubCode": "LBF",
      "coach": "Coach Name",
      "players": [
        {"name": "Kipli de Sawittos", "ovr": 90, "age": 21, "position": "FW"}
      ]
    }
  ],
  "nations": [
    {"name": "Indonesia", "logo": "indonesia.png", "code": "IDN", "coach": "National Coach",
     "players": [{"name": "Garuda One", "ovr": 78, "age": 26, "position": "FW"}]}
  ],
  "partners": [
    {"name": "Candidate Name", "age": 24, "type": "singer", "from": "Indonesia", "photo": "photo.jpg"}
  ],
  "assets": [
    {"id": "c5", "name": "Red Supercar", "image": "supercar.png"}
  ]
}
```

| Field | Required | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| `clubs[].id` | Yes | The club code in the game (see the all-clubs template) |
| `clubName` | No | Text, up to 40 letters |
| `clubLogo` | No | An image file name imported with the JSON, a data URI, an https URL, or a path inside the app |
| `clubCode` | No | 2-4 letters for the scoreboard |
| `coach` | No | Club or national coach name, up to 40 letters |
| `primary`, `secondary` | No | Hex colours, e.g. `#1E3A8A` |
| `strength` | No | 30-99 |
| `players[]` | No | `name`, `ovr` (35-99), `age` (15-45), `position` (GK, DF, MF, FW). Up to 18 per club, 26 per national team |
| `nations[].name` | Yes | The nation name in the game (as shown in the all-clubs template), plus `logo`, `code`, `coach`, `players` |
| `partners[]` | No | `name`, `age` (18-60), `type` (friend, athlete, doctor, influencer, presenter, singer, model, actress), `from`, `photo` |
| `assets[]` | No | Asset `id`, `name`, `image` |

Fields you leave out keep the default data. The database is stored on your device, used by every save slot, and can be reset to the default data at any time.

## Tips

- Use **weeks without a match** to train, and choose drills that suit your position.
- Keep a good relationship with your coach to stay in the starting line-up.
- Struggling with a low rating? Set **Opponent AI difficulty: Easy** in Settings > Match.
- Use the **Season calendar** to jump to a big match or to the end of the season.
- Shots from close range are far more likely to go in. The white line on the power bar marks the ideal power.
- Mid-range or older phone? Choose **Low** graphics for smoother play.
- Use **Export** to back up your career data.
